Engine Care

How to Check & Top Up Engine Oil

Running on low or dirty oil is the #1 cause of engine damage. Learn how to check your dipstick level and top up with genuine lubricants at home.

Monsoon Care

Biker Care Tips for Patna Monsoons

Water seepage can block spark plugs and rust drive chains. Spray WD-40 on electrical switches and clean your chain every 500km in the rainy season.

Read Tip ➔
Tires & Brakes

Improving Brake Bite & Tyre Grip

Ensure your drum brakes are adjusted and brake fluid level is clear. Maintain 25 PSI front and 35 PSI rear tyre pressure for peak mileage and grip.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about your 2-wheeler repair? Here are quick, simple answers to general doubts Patna riders ask us.

❓ How much time does a general servicing take? ▼

A standard general servicing, engine tuning, washing, and multi-point checkup takes approximately 2 to 3 hours. If you book your slot in advance, we prioritize your bike for direct consultation and express work.

❓ Do you use genuine Hero and Honda spare parts? ▼

Yes, absolutely! We use only 100% genuine and verified brand spare parts (Hero MotoCorp, Honda, TVS, Castrol, Motul lubricants). We never compromise on replacement parts to ensure your vehicle's longevity and safety.

❓ Is pickup and drop service available in Lohanipur / Patna? ▼

Yes! We offer convenient bike pickup and drop services across Patna (Lohanipur, Kadamkuan, Rajendra Nagar, Exhibition Road, etc.) for a minimal flat charge. You can select the "Pickup & Drop" option while submitting your slot booking.

❓ How does the digital tracking and payment work? ▼

Once you submit a booking, you receive a Booking Ref ID (e.g. SDH-W8A9B2). You can enter it on the website to see the live status (Received, Inspecting, Servicing, Ready). When ready, you can view the complete bill and pay directly via GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm with 0% fees!
